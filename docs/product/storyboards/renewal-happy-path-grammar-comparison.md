# Actual Ipil Assessment grammar ↔ BPLS digital working paper

This comparison uses the preserved Nelson/I pil Computation/Assessment Slip evidence only for structure and staff mental model. It intentionally excludes real taxpayer identity and does not copy source amounts.

| Actual Ipil working-paper grammar | Scenario 01 digital expression | Evidence strength |
|---|---|---|
| Business identity and transaction type at the top | `Scenario 01 Market and Kitchen` and `Renewal · 2098` lead the working paper and Assessment | **ACCEPTED** |
| Declared Lines of Business establish the computation context | Retail Trading and Food Service are first-class grouping headings | **ACCEPTED** |
| Computation rows sit under a Line of Business | Each applicable charge is shown inside its owning LOB section | **ACCEPTED** |
| Each LOB resolves to its subtotal before the next LOB | Retail `₱330.00`; Food Service `₱540.00` | **ACCEPTED** |
| Whole-business charges remain separate from LOB charges | Business Inspection Fee `₱350.00` is application-wide | **ACCEPTED** |
| Grand Total is the final financial conclusion | Evaluation and immutable Assessment both record `₱1,220.00` | **ACCEPTED** |
| Prepared By identifies the Assessment Officer | Assessment records Scenario 01 Assessment Officer and preparation time | **ACCEPTED** |
| Approved By is Municipal Treasurer authority | Exact Assessment approval records Scenario 01 Municipal Treasurer | **ACCEPTED** |
| Schedule of payments follows the assessed liability | Payment Schedule `7` is a separate pending payable with balance `₱1,220.00` | **ACCEPTED** for single payable; installments **BLOCKED** |
| Paper annotations convey office/rule provenance | Digital rows show responsibility and provenance as secondary detail | **PROVISIONAL UAT** presentation |
| Production formulas and prices are statutory | Only Business Inspection Fee `₱350.00` is an accepted governed rule; all six scenario contributions are explicitly `provisional_uat` | **BLOCKED** for commissioning synthetic prices |

The product preserves the mental model, not the paper styling: **Business → Renewal → LOB → computation rows → subtotal → next LOB → application-wide → Grand Total → Prepared By → Approved By**.
