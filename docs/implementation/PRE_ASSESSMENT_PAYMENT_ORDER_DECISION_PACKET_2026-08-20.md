# Pre-Assessment Paperless Payment Order Decision Packet

Status: **MUNICIPAL DECISION REQUIRED — IMPLEMENTATION HELD**

Source: `OPERATIONAL-NELSON-001`

## Confirmed source fact

During Step 1, the applicant secures Paperless Payment Orders from the MPDO, Engineering Office, and Municipal Assessor’s Office, plus any `OTHER OFFICE`. Completion of all required payment orders precedes automatic forwarding to Step 2.

The source does not define the object or its lifecycle. TOR and studied legacy evidence do not establish a matching canonical payment-order model.

## Decisions required

1. What is a Paperless Payment Order: departmental fee instruction, preliminary assessment, payable order, documentary endorsement, approval, or another municipal record?
2. Does it contain an amount, fee lines, legal basis, payer, application identity, validity period, reference number, or supporting documents?
3. Do its amounts become inputs to the Step 2 final assessment, or are they merely prerequisites/evidence?
4. Which payment orders apply by application type, business activity, legal form, location, risk, or other condition?
5. Who creates, approves, corrects, cancels, expires, and completes each order?
6. Does `completion` mean issued, approved, acknowledged, paid, or another state?
7. What happens if an office returns, changes, or withdraws an order after Step 2 begins?
8. Is automatic forwarding mandatory system behavior? If so, what exact event, retry, failure, notification, audit, and override rules apply?
9. Is `MPDO` intentionally distinct from post-payment `MPDC`, and what office names/codes are authoritative?
10. What does `OTHER OFFICE` mean, and who configures applicability without inventing fiscal policy?

## Explicit non-decisions

This packet does not authorize a new fee calculation path, department billing subsystem, payment collection, final assessment, documentary sufficiency decision, clearance completion, or automatic workflow transition.
