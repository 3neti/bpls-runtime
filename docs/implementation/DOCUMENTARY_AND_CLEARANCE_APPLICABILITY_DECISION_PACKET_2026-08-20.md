## Decisions required

1. Is `Proof of Registration` an umbrella for DTI, SEC, or CDA evidence according to legal form, or a separate document?
2. Is `Barangay Clearance` the same municipal concept as `Barangay Business Clearance`?
3. Which documents apply to new, renewal, additional, amendment, transfer, and retirement/closure transactions?
4. Which legal forms require DTI, SEC, CDA, or combinations, and what accepted aliases/documents satisfy each requirement?
5. When are ITR, CTC, and Sworn Statement required; which year/version is valid; and are they assessment inputs, supporting evidence, or both?
6. Which clearances are universal versus activity-, location-, risk-, occupancy-, or application-type dependent?
7. Is Health Clearance equivalent to the legacy Sanitary Clearance, and is FSIC the accepted Fire Safety clearance identity?
8. Who decides applicability, deficiency, approval, expiry, waiver, correction, and sufficiency for each document and clearance?
9. What immutable audit evidence must record each office approval and any return/correction?
10. Are clearances always initiated only after full payment, or do inspection/preparation activities begin earlier while approval remains post-payment?
