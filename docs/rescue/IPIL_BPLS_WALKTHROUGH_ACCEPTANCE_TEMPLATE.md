## Troubleshooting and stop conditions

1. Do not invent a value or skip ahead.
2. Confirm the actor and normal-login identity.
3. Confirm Historical Review versus Current Workflow environment.
4. Confirm the exact tracking/search value and active filters.
5. Refresh once; do not blindly resubmit a material action.
6. Verify the previous actor completed the final confirmation.
7. Verify the expected subtotal/total before continuing.
8. Record URL, actor, expected, actual and a credential-free screenshot privately.
9. Stop at the first material financial, authorization or state defect. Preserve the record for review.

**Immediate stops:** wrong financial total; missing receipt group; unexpected cross-office authority; unverified credential; historical operational action; unexpected public historical access; duplicate/conflicting confirmation; current specimen not prepared; missing accepted fee/numbering authority. Do not repair these during the session.

For historical discrepancies: **Record first. Do not repair during the walkthrough.** Unavailable documents are findings, not permission to attach unresolved files. Request reports and policy decisions as findings; do not reconstruct reports or accept mappings live.
