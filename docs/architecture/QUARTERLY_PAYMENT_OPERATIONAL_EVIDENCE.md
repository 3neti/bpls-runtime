# Quarterly Payment — Operational Evidence Packet

Evidence `OPERATIONAL-IPIL-ASSESSMENT-001` and `OPERATIONAL-IPIL-ASSESSMENT-002` visibly records a Quarterly payment mode with Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4 rows, due dates, amounts, and balances.

Classification: municipality-supplied operational evidence.  
Decision: preserve the existing downstream boundary `Evaluation → Assessment → Treasurer decision → PaymentSchedule`.

This evidence does not commission an allocation formula. The visible first-quarter amount is not a simple grand-total division by four in either example. Component cadence, first-quarter loading, due-date policy, rounding, balance behavior, and exceptions remain municipal fiscal questions. The Evaluator conformance packet therefore creates/uses a PaymentSchedule only to prove the post-Assessment financial mutation lock; it does not calculate or infer Q1–Q4 allocations.
