| Step | Process / Stage | Requirements / Activities | Responsible Office / Person | System Action / Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| STEP 1 | APPLICATION | Applicant submits Barangay Clearance and Proof of Registration. Applicant will also secure Paperless Payment Orders from the MPDO, Engineering Office, and Municipal Assessor’s Office. | Applicant, MPDO, Engineering Office, Municipal Assessor’s Office, OTHER OFFICE | Upon completion of all required payment orders, the application is automatically forwarded to Step 2 – One-Time Assessment and Approval. |
| STEP 2 | ONE-TIME ASSESSMENT AND APPROVAL | Applicant submits ITR (Income Tax Return) CTC and Sworn Statement. Assessment of all applicable business taxes, regulatory fees, and other charges is performed. | Assessment Officer / Approving Officer | Once the assessment is completed and approved, the application proceeds to One-Time Payment. |
|  | ONE-TIME PAYMENT OF ALL ASSESSED FEES | Applicant pays all assessed business taxes, regulatory fees, and other applicable charges in one transaction. After payment, the applicant secures the required clearances: MPDC Clearance, Engineering Clearance, MENRO Clearance, Health Clearance, and Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC). | Applicant, Municipal Treasurer’s Office, MPDC, Engineering, MENRO, Health Office, BFP, OTHER OFFICE | Once all required clearances are approved by their respective offices, the application is approved and pushed to the Business Permit Portal for release. |
| STEP 3 | BUSINESS PERMIT RELEASE | Applicant proceeds to the Business Permit Licensing Office to secure/retrieve the approved Business Permit. | BPLO / Releasing Officer | Business Permit is released/issued to the applicant. |
