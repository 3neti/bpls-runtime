# General

## Keep historical calibration separate from policy
Golden Financial Specimens verify five-layer historical evidence without evaluating formulas or activating policy. Historical reproduction assertions and future-policy assertions are separate; accepted policy requires explicit municipal authority evidence and remains unexecuted by the calibration suite. Preserve conflicting historical values and never infer missing fee identity from names.
