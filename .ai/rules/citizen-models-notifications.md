# Citizen Models Notifications

## Keep citizen tracking separate from official numbering
Formal citizen receipt may assign a stable non-sensitive tracking reference and one factual in-app receipt notice. The tracking reference is not an official application number, and notice wording must not imply documentary sufficiency, assessment, approval, permit issuance, or external delivery.
