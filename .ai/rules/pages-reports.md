# Pages Reports

## Reports project evidence and preserve authority
Operational reports project persisted domain evidence and never recalculate liability. Authority-bearing outputs must refuse rows and exports when required legal or municipal authority facts are unresolved; a raw status or generated artifact is not issuance/release evidence. Both categories share normal Laravel reporting infrastructure.
